About Dr. Avinash Kasundra

Dr. Avinash Kasundra is a Consultant Intensivist and Pulmonologist practicing at Premier Hospital & ICU, Rajkot. His clinical career focuses on delivering specialized intensive care resuscitation and comprehensive outpatient respiratory medicine.
